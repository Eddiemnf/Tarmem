Tarmem: interface and journey evaluation

A walk-through of the live build in both languages and both roles — landing page, sign-up, homeowner path and contractor path. Fourteen findings, ordered by what costs you users first.

What is already working

Sign-up is the strongest flow in the product. Three steps, a progress bar that fills right-to-left correctly in Arabic, the demo OTP printed on screen, and both resend and change number available at the point of failure. Nothing about it needs defending.

The Nafath gating language is placed well and says the right thing: verification is required at the moment of commitment, not at the door. That is a real conversion decision and the copy carries it.

The project workspace tab model — overview, bids, stages, messages, files, payments — holds up under both roles, and the counts on the tabs do useful work. The agreement gate between bid acceptance and contractor assignment is legible and correctly sequenced.

RTL is handled properly everywhere except the one case noted below: logical properties throughout, mirrored tables, Western digits for currency, which is right for Saudi users.

Blocking

Fix before anyone else sees it

01 · The English homepage has an Arabic headline

Switch to EN and the hero still reads البيت اللي تحلم فيه يبدأ من مقاول تثق فيه. The subhead, the kicker, the input label and the chips all translate; the h1 does not, because the Arabic string is typed directly into the template instead of coming from the string file. The translated headlines still exist in tarmem-i18n.js under home.heroWords — nothing reads them any more.

Fix: move the headline back into the string file with an en and an ar value, and render it through the same path as the subhead.

02 · Dates print backwards in Arabic

On the project overview, تاريخ النشر reads Aug 2026 12. A Latin month name dropped into an RTL cell gets reordered by the bidirectional algorithm, so the day lands after the year. The same field appears on every browse card, which is the first thing a contractor reads when deciding whether a job is fresh.

Fix: give the Arabic locale real Arabic month names, and wrap any remaining mixed-script date in a bidi isolate so it can never reorder.

03 · Signed-in homeowners lose the post-a-project button

A guest gets انشر مشروعك as a filled primary button in the header on every page. Sign in as a homeowner and the header collapses to two text links — dashboard and browse contractors — and the primary action of the entire product is now reachable only from the dashboard. The person who has already committed gets less help than the stranger.

Fix: keep the button in the homeowner header. It is the same component; it just needs to survive the role switch.

Should fix

Costing you trust or clicks

04 · The landing page asks for a decision without offering evidence

The page runs hero → four how-it-works cards → two sign-up cards → footer. Between the promise and the ask there is no proof: no count of verified contractors, no fee stated, no example of a finished project, no answer to the question a first-time visitor actually has, which is what happens to my money. The simplification pass cut the trust strip but left its copy behind — ١٠٠٪ مقاولون موثّقون بنفاذ, ٣ مراحل دفع, ٥٪ رسوم خدمة فقط are all still in the string file, unreferenced.

Fix: one hairline-ruled strip of three facts under the hero. The copy is written; it needs eight lines of markup. Anything more than that and you are back where you started.

05 · One page, two names

The header calls it كيف يعمل. The footer calls it طريقة العمل. Both navigate to the same route. Pick one and use it in both places — a returning user should not have to work out that these are the same destination.

06 · The hero headline does not scale

It is set at a fixed 52px with a line-height of 1.43 while every other heading in the build uses a fluid clamp. Below roughly 900px it crowds the AI input directly beneath it, and 1.43 is loose enough for display type that the two lines read as separate thoughts rather than one sentence. Match the clamp pattern used elsewhere and tighten the leading to about 1.15.

07 · The demo role switcher is invisible

At the bottom of sign-up, تصفّح المنصة بصفتك is a collapsed disclosure with no caret and no underline, so it reads as an unfinished sentence rather than a control. Anyone demoing the product will miss the fastest way into it. Add a caret and a hover state, or expand it by default.

08 · Browse shows three pagination signals for one list

Under the open-projects grid sit a load more +N button, a thin progress bar, and a showing X of Y counter — three ways of saying the same thing, stacked. Keep the button and the counter; drop the bar.

Polish

Worth a pass when there is time

  1. Both dashboards lead with four near-identical numbers. The homeowner sees 1 active, 1 bid received, 1 awaiting approval. At this data scale the row spends the most valuable strip on the page to say very little. Consider one sentence plus the attention list.
  2. Project overview repeats itself. Budget and status appear in the page header and again in the details table two hundred pixels below.
  3. A zero presented as a metric. The homeowner profile shows ٠ خلافات قائمة alongside real stats; absence of disputes is better said in words than scored.
  4. The hero changes register. اللي تحلم فيه and وش تجدّد في بيتك are colloquial; every screen after it is MSA. The warmth is deliberate and works, but the seam is abrupt — carry a little of it into the first screen after sign-up, or let the hero settle closer to the rest.
Code health

Left behind by the redesign

13 · Dead work on every paint

trustRow, heroWordEl, heroTailEl, heroCard and heroPick are computed on every render and nothing reads them. .v-duo, .v-strip and .v-trust are styled but never applied. The matching strings sit unused in the string file. None of it is visible, all of it still costs.

14 · A timer re-renders the homepage every three seconds for nothing

The rotating-headline interval is still running on the home route, calling setState on a three-second cycle to advance a word that is no longer rendered. Clear it, or restore the rotation deliberately — right now it is a full re-render loop with no output.

Checked in Arabic and English, as guest, homeowner and contractor, against Tarmem.dc.html as of 11 September 2026. Inner pages beyond the two dashboards and the project workspace — admin, messaging detail, wallet — were reviewed but not walked end to end; they are still on the pre-redesign visual language and will need their own pass.
